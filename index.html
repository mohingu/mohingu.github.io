About Me

I'm a Lead Data Scientist with over six years' experience building and shipping machine learning in insurance. At Zego I lead the operationalisation of ML across pricing and data science - owning the SageMaker infrastructure, automated retraining, and the production framework that takes models from notebook to live deployment.

Previously, at Marshmallow, I built and owned a suite of pricing GBMs that scaled across new business and generated over £130m in written premium. Across my career I've delivered models end to end - pricing, risk, and the MLOps and engineering that keep them running reliably in production.

My foundation is in mathematics (MSc, Durham University), and I care as much about the systems around a model as the model itself. Outside of work I'm focused on AI and building things - exploring where machine learning meets real, useful products.
